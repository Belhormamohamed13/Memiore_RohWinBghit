\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\thispagestyle{plain}

This thesis presents the design and development of RohWinBghit, an intelligent mobile carpooling platform designed to address the challenges of inter-wilaya transportation in Algeria. The work responds to a significant societal need arising from the lack of a secure, reliable, and trusted digital solution for long-distance travel across the country. Existing transportation options are often characterized by high costs, limited availability, and insufficient mechanisms to establish trust between passengers and drivers. The proposed platform aims to improve the accessibility, safety, and efficiency of shared mobility services by integrating features that facilitate trip planning, ride booking, real-time communication, and journey monitoring. Particular attention is given to user trust and security through the implementation of identity verification mechanisms, fraud prevention measures, and secure transaction management. The platform also incorporates intelligent functionalities to support decision-making and enhance the overall user experience. A comprehensive system architecture was designed and implemented to ensure scalability, reliability, and performance. The developed solution supports real-time interactions between users, location-based services, dynamic trip management, and multiple payment options adapted to the Algerian context. The effectiveness of the platform was evaluated through extensive testing, including automated validation of core functionalities, integration testing, and user-interface verification. The obtained results demonstrate the robustness of the proposed solution and its ability to meet the functional and security requirements of a modern carpooling service. Overall, this work contributes to the digital transformation of transportation services in Algeria by proposing a practical and innovative mobility solution that promotes safer, more accessible, and more sustainable inter-wilaya travel.
